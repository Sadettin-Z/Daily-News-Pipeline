### 2. French high school protests enter third week as government concessions fail to calm students

France's high school protests over teacher shortages, overcrowded classes and decrepit schools are in their third week, with 71,500 people marching nationwide on October 8 according to the Interior Ministry and 224 lycées fully closed. Police tactics have become a driving force of the protests after 6,059 arrests between September 28 and October 5, mostly of minors, and serious injuries including a 15-year-old whose hand was torn off by a stun grenade; the government has since banned stun grenades.
