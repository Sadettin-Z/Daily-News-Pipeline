### 2. Democrats seize Senate and House as voters punish Trump over war and prices

Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress in Tuesday's midterm elections, reaching the 51 Senate seats needed and heading for a large House majority; Welt's reading of the Associated Press interim count projects 255 House seats to the Republicans' 173 and a Senate of 56 to 44 counting two independents. They flipped seven Republican-held Senate seats, in Texas, Ohio, Kansas, Iowa, North Carolina, South Carolina and Maine, and held off most of the Republican mid-decade redistricting, keeping at least eight of the 15 targeted House districts while picking up seats in California and Utah. The CNN Voter Poll shows Trump's approval below 40% nationally, three-quarters of voters calling economic conditions bad and more than 60% blaming higher costs on his policies; the Iran war that sent fuel prices soaring, tariffs and the cost of living dominated the vote, and during his monthlong salvage mission of rallies Trump himself kept asking a close associate "How did we get here?". Trump told The New York Times "I am absolutely willing to accept those results", and his voter-fraud crusade never materialized on Election Day after the Supreme Court, federal courts and local officials blocked its drastic steps. Democrats can now frustrate his agenda and launch investigations in both chambers, and a historic third impeachment attempt could be on the cards; Abdul El-Sayed won Michigan to become the first Muslim US senator.

### 6. Spain's transport minister Puente resigns six days after crash that killed 69

Óscar Puente resigned on Tuesday evening at the end of a lengthy congressional hearing on the 28 October high-speed collision near Urda, south of Madrid, in which a Renfe train ran into a stationary Iryo train and 69 people died, Spain's worst rail crash since 2013 and one of Europe's worst this century. "A transport minister in a country like Spain cannot remain in office after two accidents of this seriousness in less than a year," he said, referring also to January's crash in Andalusia that killed 46; authorities blame a combination of human and computer errors, and Puente said a train was authorized to proceed past a red signal "under inexplicable circumstances". He had resisted calls to quit for days, presented new safety measures hours earlier, and also withdrew from the Socialist lists for the 29 November snap election, in which Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez, who had stood by him, is trailing in the polls; Sánchez said "politicians like him bring dignity to our country". Sumar, the junior coalition partner, and the entire opposition had demanded his resignation.

"2027 Ocak ayından itibaren geçerli olması hedeflenen mevcut yasal artışlara ilave iyileştirmeleri içeren bir çalışma yapıyoruz" diyen Işıkhan, Asgari Ücret Tespit Komisyonu'nun Aralık ayında toplanacağını belirtti.

### Özel Kılıçdaroğlu'nu neden eleştirdi, Emir Sarıgül'ün iddialarına ne yanıt verdi?

Yeni Parti Genel Başkanı Özgür Özel, TBMM Grup Toplantısı'nda 21 Mayıs'ta mutlak butlan kararıyla CHP Genel Başkanlığı'na getirilen Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu'nu anayasa değişikliği ve dokunulmazlıkların kaldırılması konusundaki tutumu nedeniyle eleştirdi: "Butlancılar şimdi 'Anayasa'yı değiştirmeye varım' diyor. Verilmiş sözler şimdi çıkıyor teker teker." Özel, Kılıçdaroğlu'nun "Mehmetçik'i çöllere yollarım", "Erdoğan'ın Trump'la yaptığı anlaşmada ben de varım" ve dokunulmazlıkları kaldırmak için anayasa değiştireceğini söylediğini öne sürdü.
